What am I looking at?

  • Live mode means this page is talking to boxoffice serve. Pressing Project runs real read-only ClickHouse aggregation and every dot and number is filled from that response.
  • Recorded mode means the page was opened as a file with no backend, so the Ask panel replays a labelled transcript captured from a real run. The projector needs a live backend.
  • The crowd is the actual comparable titles in the chosen genre and year, placed by their rating. The band is the mean plus or minus one spread across that whole cohort.
  • The multiverse rail flips the same scenario across years and across sibling genres. Each flip re-projects a new real cohort.
  • Under the hood holds the load-bearing proof: the exact SQL, the grounding trace, the transport, and the guardrail audit. Always one click away, never faked.
